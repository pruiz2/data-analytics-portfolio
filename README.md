## Projects

### [Northwind Sales Analysis](https://github.com/pruiz2/data-analytics-portfolio/blob/main/northwind-sales-analysis)
**SQL | MySQL**
Business analysis of the Northwind database exploring sales performance, 
customer behavior, employee productivity, and supplier revenue across 
10 business questions.

---

### [IBM HR Attrition Analysis](https://github.com/pruiz2/data-analytics-portfolio/blob/main/hr-attrition-excel-analysis)
**Excel | Dashboard**
Analysis of 1,470 IBM employees to identify key drivers of employee 
attrition. Found that overtime employees are 3x more likely to leave 
and Sales Representatives have a 40% attrition rate — the highest of 
any role.

---

### [Video Game Sales Analysis](https://github.com/pruiz2/data-analytics-portfolio/blob/main/vgsales-excel-analysis)
**Excel | Dashboard**
Analysis of 16,000+ video games across genres and platforms to identify 
market trends and sales efficiency. Found that Shooter games have the 
highest average sales per game despite ranking 4th in total published titles.

---

### [Retail Sales Analysis](https://github.com/pruiz2/data-analytics-portfolio/blob/main/retail-sales-analysis)
**Python | pandas | matplotlib**
EDA on 1,000 retail transactions to uncover spending patterns across 
gender, age groups, and product categories.

---

### [Spotify Top Hits Analysis](https://github.com/pruiz2/data-analytics-portfolio/blob/main/spotify-analysis)
**SQL | Python**
Analysis of 2,000+ Spotify tracks to identify what makes a song popular 
and how music has changed from 2000–2019.
